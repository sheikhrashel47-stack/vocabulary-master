## Chosen Direction — Scholar’s Ledger

**Design Movement:** Contemporary academic editorial design, informed by a refined study ledger and modern mobile reference tools.

**Core Principles:** Calm reading first; one clear action per surface; visible hierarchy through typography and spacing; meaningful empty states instead of fabricated achievement data.

**Color Philosophy:** Near-white paper and pale mineral green make long reading easy, while deep emerald is reserved for orientation, selected states and intentional actions. Warm graphite keeps Bengali and English content readable without stark black.

**Layout Paradigm:** A narrow mobile reading column with a pinned utility header and bottom navigation rail. Desktop expands into a quiet two-column reference desk rather than stretching mobile cards edge-to-edge.

**Signature Elements:** An emerald ledger rule under active titles; small indexed alphabet markers; softly squared word cards with a semantic meaning/synonym/antonym rhythm.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Navigation and search respond immediately. Buttons have a restrained press response, while locked tools clearly explain their status instead of pretending to work.

**Animation:** Motion is limited to 160–220ms opacity and transform transitions. Search and tab changes are immediate. Motion is removed for reduced-motion users.

**Typography System:** Newsreader provides English display/word reading texture; Noto Sans Bengali ensures unclipped Bengali meanings; Manrope supplies compact labels, navigation and numbers. Large words are serif-led; controls use the precise sans system.

**Brand Essence:** A vocabulary-first study desk for admission learners who want a clear, calm path from word to mastery. **Personality:** scholarly, composed, precise.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are direct and quietly motivating; microcopy explains the current state without hype. Example lines: “A word at a time, with purpose.” and “Your vocabulary desk is ready when you are.”

**Wordmark & Logo:** An abstract emerald bookmark formed by three stepped ledger lines, suggesting a saved place in a book without relying on the brand name as the mark.

**Signature Brand Color:** Ledger Emerald — `#176B56`.

## Style Decisions

- No fake progress, streak, XP, mastery or activity statistics in Phase‑1.
- Every learning, parser, testing, reward, AI, notification, API and sync tool remains visibly locked or absent.
- The dashboard stays sparse; Vocabulary Bank is the primary active destination.
- Desktop is a deliberate two-column academic reference desk, never a mobile card stack floating in empty space.
- Newsreader owns display and word moments; Manrope owns labels and actions. Ledger Emerald `#176B56` recurs as the rule, action and indexed-marker color.
